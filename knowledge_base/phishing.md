# Phishing

## Overview

Phishing is a social-engineering technique in which an attacker attempts to trick users into revealing credentials, financial information, or other sensitive information.

## Common Indicators

- Suspicious or unexpected emails
- Requests for passwords or sensitive information
- Links pointing to unfamiliar domains
- Urgent or threatening language
- Unexpected attachments
- Sender-domain inconsistencies

## Potential Impact

Phishing can lead to:
- Credential theft
- Account compromise
- Malware delivery
- Financial loss
- Unauthorized access
- Data exposure

## Defensive Response

1. Preserve the original email and relevant headers.
2. Avoid interacting with suspicious links or attachments.
3. Identify suspicious domains and URLs.
4. Check whether credentials were submitted.
5. Reset compromised credentials when necessary.
6. Enforce MFA.
7. Block confirmed malicious indicators.
8. Monitor affected accounts.

## Investigation Priorities

Determine:
- Who received the message
- Whether the message was opened
- Whether links or attachments were accessed
- Whether credentials were submitted
- Whether additional users received the same message
